PROTOTYPE — the live classification above is an illustrative simulation of the panel, not the validated classifier. Real regulatory directions are encoded for the genes the study reports (PTGDS, FADS2, IL5RA down in schizophrenia; FADS2 and HADHA up in bipolar); the remaining weights are illustrative to demonstrate the workflow.
