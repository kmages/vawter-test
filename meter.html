The SZ Meter reads one number off the blood draw.

From the 18-gene signature, the classifier returns a schizophrenia probability. The meter is where a clinician reads it: the needle rises through a scale that is green where the sample looks like a healthy control, gold through the bipolar-overlap zone, and indigo where it is consistent with schizophrenia. Colour and position carry the same call.
