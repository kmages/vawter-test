The bench unit that runs the draw and shows the meter.

A benchtop analyzer sized for a clinic lab. A drawn blood tube goes into a single-use cartridge, the cartridge goes in the bay, and one button starts the run. The 18-gene panel is read on board and the result is shown where the clinician stands: the SZ Meter, live on the front screen.
